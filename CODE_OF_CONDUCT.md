# Code of Conduct

SonicNest contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexual harassment, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not accepted. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations.

Report project conduct concerns privately to `supportramsandesh@gmail.com` with enough context for review.
